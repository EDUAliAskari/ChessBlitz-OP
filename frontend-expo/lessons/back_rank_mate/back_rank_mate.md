# Back Rank Mate — Explanation

## Instruction

You know the trick now. This is called “back rank mate.”

Checkmate is the ultimate goal, so as long as you get it you win. How can you use what you’ve learned to win, at any cost?

**PUZZLE**

## Feedback

At the cost of your poor queen, you win the game! Remember, so long as you checkmate, nothing else matters. It’s the ultimate goal of the game! Even if it takes a piece or two to get there.
